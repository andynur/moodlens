# Code of Conduct

Moodlens adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other channel that represents the project.

## In short

We want a friendly and harassment-free community for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

Expected behavior:

- Be kind and patient, especially with students and first-time contributors.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or sexual language and imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Publishing other people's private information, including student names, IDs or course data, without permission.
- Any other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@andynur](https://github.com/andynur). If the matter is sensitive, use the private channel described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) and say that the report is about conduct.

The maintainer reviews every report, keeps the reporter's identity private, and decides on action under the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant. Actions range from a private warning to a permanent ban from the project.
