## Positioning

**We are:** the integrity-safe, local-first study companion for any Moodle site.

**We are not:** a cheating tool, a downloader, a Moodle plugin, or an official university product.

## Value pillars for store listing and README

1. Everything due, in one place (tasks, reminders, calendar export).
2. Your Moodle, your way (dashboard, colors, dark mode, what's new).
3. Practice that sticks (flashcards, spaced repetition, review your mistakes, XP and streaks).
4. Private by default (no account, no server, open source).
5. Safe for academic integrity (no help on live quizzes, no LMS writes).
6. English and Bahasa Indonesia.

## Distribution ideas

- Lecturer-facing one-page guide explaining integrity safeguards, so lecturers can recommend it.
